**Student:** what is the purpose of the exact solution function

**Claude:** It's an answer key for checking your simulation.

Your real model will eventually have microbes, nickel and isotopes, and no one can solve that exactly with a formula. The only way to get answers is to simulate: take many small steps forward in time. The simple case, with a steady volcanic source and burial and nothing else, has an exact formula:

```

```

So you can:

1. Run your simulation on that simple case.
2. Compare it to `exact_solution` at every time step.
3. If they match, your stepping code is right. Now you can trust it when you add the complicated parts that have no formula.
